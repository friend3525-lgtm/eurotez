Return Policy

At Eurotez, we want you to have a satisfactory experience. If for any reason you are not happy with your purchase, we accept returns under the following conditions:

01

You have 14 days from the delivery date to request a return.

02

We only accept unopened, unused products in their original packaging for safety and hygiene reasons.

03

Manage your return through our email or contact form available on this page.

04

Refunds: Once the product has been received and verified, we will process your refund within 5 to 7 business days using the same payment method used.

05

If you receive a damaged or defective product, please notify us within the first 48 hours after delivery with photos of the product so we can send you a replacement or refund at no extra cost.

06

Original shipping costs are refundable only in the case of damaged products or errors in the order. The customer is responsible for return shipping in all other cases.

Return Process

01

Contact us via email at llc@eurotezskin.com. Include your order number, the reason for the return, and whether the product is unopened and in its original packaging.

02

Wait for our response within 24-48 hours, we will send you the return address and an authorization number.

03

Pack the product and return it in its original packaging, well protected to avoid damage during shipping.

04

Send the product to the address indicated. The shipping cost is the customer's responsibility, except if the product arrived damaged or there was an error in the order.

05

Refund: Once received and verified, we will process the refund within 5-7 business days.
