What’s Inside, and Why

Every ingredient earns its place. Here is what each one does for your skin.

02
1

Ceramides

Ceramides are a natural part of your skin’s outer layer, where they help hold moisture in. Eurotez includes a blend of ceramides to help support that moisture barrier, so skin feels comfortable, not tight.

2

Squalane

A lightweight oil similar to the oils your skin makes naturally. It locks in moisture and leaves skin soft without a heavy or greasy feel.

3

Urea

A moisture-binding ingredient your skin already knows. It draws water to the skin’s surface and helps smooth dry, rough patches.

4

Centella Asiatica

A botanical long used in skincare for its calming properties. It helps comfort skin and keep it looking balanced.

5

Vitamin E

An antioxidant that helps protect skin from everyday stress and keeps it looking revitalized. On the label it appears as tocopherol.

6

Plant Oils and Shea Butter

Jojoba, meadowfoam, coconut, and sea buckthorn oils, with shea butter, nourish dry skin and leave it soft and supple.

Also in the jar

Glycerin and panthenol (provitamin B5) for extra moisture, plus phytosteryl oleate and hydrogenated lecithin to help keep skin soft and comfortable.
